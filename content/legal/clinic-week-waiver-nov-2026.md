This waiver applies to everyone taking part in **Clinic Week, 1 to 5 November
2026**, including Two-Day Pass holders. It covers the welcome dinner on
1 November and the on-court sessions from 2 to 5 November at **Arise Pickleball
(Udom Suk)** and **Papaya Pickleball Club**, Bangkok, led by Coach Travis Rhea
(Mind Your Pickle), Jaron, and Ryan.

It sits alongside our [Terms and Conditions](/terms), which cover booking,
payment, and cancellation. In this waiver, "we" and "TPP" mean The Pickleball
Passport.

Please read it in full. You confirm you agree to it by ticking the waiver box
and signing your traveler intake form.

---

## 1. The risks

Pickleball is a physical, fast-moving sport. Taking part in Clinic Week carries
risks, including but not limited to:

- sprains, strains, muscle and tendon injuries (including Achilles injuries)
- falls, fractures, and head injuries
- being struck by a ball or paddle, including eye injuries
- heat exhaustion, heat stroke, and dehydration in Bangkok heat and humidity
- heart and other medical events brought on by exertion
- collisions with other players, nets, fences, or court fixtures
- slips on wet or uneven surfaces
- food allergies or illness at the welcome dinner or other meals
- road and travel risks getting to and from the venues

Some of these risks cannot be removed, even with care from everyone involved.

## 2. Your fitness and health

By taking part you confirm that:

- you are physically fit to play pickleball for up to three hours a day in hot
  conditions
- you have told us, on your intake form or in writing before your first
  session, about any medical condition, injury, medication, allergy, pregnancy,
  or mobility limitation that could affect your participation or safety
- you have taken medical advice where you are unsure whether you should play
- you will stop, rest, and tell a coach straight away if you feel unwell,
  dizzy, short of breath, or in pain

We are not medical providers and do not supervise your health.

## 3. Assumption of risk

You take part in Clinic Week voluntarily and **at your own risk**. You
understand the risks described above and accept them.

## 4. Safety instructions and conduct

You agree to follow the instructions of our coaches and the rules of each
venue, and to use appropriate court shoes and equipment. Protective eyewear is
recommended.

We may stop you from taking part in any drill, game, or session where we
reasonably believe it is unsafe for you or others. No refund is due in that
case.

## 5. Emergency medical treatment

If you are injured or become unwell and cannot make decisions for yourself, you
authorise TPP and venue staff to arrange first aid, emergency transport, and
medical treatment on your behalf.

All medical, hospital, and transport costs are yours. Clinic Week does not
include insurance of any kind. We strongly recommend travel and medical
insurance that covers sport.

To the fullest extent permitted by law, you release TPP, its owners, coaches,
staff, and partner venues (including Arise Pickleball and Papaya Pickleball
Club) from claims for injury, illness, loss, or damage arising from the
ordinary, inherent risks of taking part in Clinic Week described in this
waiver.

**This release does not apply to** death or bodily injury caused by our
negligence, to gross negligence or wilful misconduct, or to any liability that
cannot be limited or excluded under the laws of Thailand.

## 7. Your belongings

You are responsible for your paddles, bags, phones, and other belongings at the
venues and in transit. We are not liable for loss, theft, or damage.

## 8. Photos and video

Your photo and video preference on your intake form applies to Clinic Week. If
you choose not to be featured, we will not use images of you in public

## 9. Who can sign

You must be 18 or over to agree to this waiver. If a participant is under 18, a
parent or legal guardian must agree to it and sign on their behalf.
